Photo, screenshot, PDF or link: your chord sheets come in as one tidy format you can check and edit, in your private songbook. Plus a tuner that shows which peg to turn.

  • For iPhone and iPad
  • One-time purchase
  • Coming soon to the App Store

X Guitar is your private songbook: every chord sheet you play, in one clean format, kept on your device or in your own iCloud. With a tuner you can trust built in.

Tuner

  • Pluck a string and it finds the one you meant — or pin a string yourself when one has drifted far out of tune.
  • Tells you which way to turn: tighten or loosen, and which peg, drawn on your headstock.
  • Standard tuning plus Drop D, Open G, DADGAD, half-step down and more, or define your own.
  • Reference tone, adjustable A4, sensitivity for noisy rooms, and a large display you can read from a music stand.
  • Put a capo on and the targets follow.

Adding sheets

  • Photograph or import a screenshot or PDF of a chord sheet and X Guitar reads it on your device — no network, no account, no upload. It gives you a draft to check and fix, not a promise of perfection.
  • Paste a link to a page that already has a chord sheet on it and the sheet comes straight in.
  • If you have your own API key for a model provider, you can choose to use it for harder pages. That is entirely optional, always asks first, and sends the image only to the provider you named.

Chord sheets

  • Chords sit above the words, where they belong. Chord diagrams for every shape in the song.
  • Change the key to the one you actually sing in — the app then suggests which shapes to play and where to put the capo, preferring shapes you already know.
  • Performance mode scrolls the sheet at your pace, hands free.
  • Search by title, artist, or the opening line of the lyrics. Group songs into setlists for a gig.
  • Your sheets are plain ChordPro files. Import them, export them, keep them. iCloud sync is optional and uses your own account.

Privacy

No account. No analytics. No third-party SDKs. The tuner never records. Recognition runs on your device. The app touches the network in exactly three places, and each one only after you ask it to.

调音器 + 你的私人弹唱谱库

拍照、截图、PDF 或一条链接，都能认成统一格式、可以核对修改的弹唱谱，收进你的私人谱库。再配一个会告诉你往哪边拧的调音器。

  • iPhone 与 iPad
  • 一次买断
  • 即将登陆 App Store

X Guitar 是你的私人弹唱谱库：你弹的每一首谱，统一成一种干净的格式，存在你自己的设备或你自己的 iCloud 里。再配一个信得过的调音器。

调音器

  • 拨一下，它自己认出你要调哪根；某根跑得太离谱时，也可以自己点死一根。
  • 告诉你往哪边拧——松还是紧、哪个弦钮，画在你的琴头上。
  • 标准调弦之外还有 Drop D、Open G、DADGAD、降半音等，也可以自己定义。
  • 参考音、可调 A4、嘈杂环境下的灵敏度，以及谱架上看得清的大字模式。
  • 夹上变调夹，目标音自动跟着变。

加谱子

  • 拍一张、或选一张截图 / PDF，识别在你的设备上完成——不联网、不上传、不要账号。它给你的是一份要你核对和修改的草稿，不是「一定准」的承诺。
  • 页面上本来就有和弦谱的，粘一条链接就直接取进来。
  • 你要是有自己的模型服务商 API key，也可以选择用它来啃更难的页面。这一步完全可选、每次都先问过你，而且只发给你自己指定的那个服务商。

弹唱谱

  • 和弦压在词上面，本来就该在的位置。本曲用到的每个和弦都配指法图。
  • 换成你实际唱得上去的调——然后它会告诉你该用哪套指法、变调夹夹几品，并优先推荐你已经会的指法族。
  • 演出模式按你的速度滚动，不用腾手。
  • 按歌名、歌手，或者歌词的第一句搜。可以把歌编进歌单，一场演出一份。
  • 你的谱就是普通的 ChordPro 文件，导得进也导得出。iCloud 同步是可选的，走你自己的账户。

隐私

没有账号，没有统计，没有任何第三方 SDK。调音器从不录音。识别在你的设备上跑。这个 App 只有三处会联网，每一处都要你先动手。
